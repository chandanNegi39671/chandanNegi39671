## About Me

I am a final-year B.Tech Computer Science student (Expected 2027, CGPA 8.3/10) specializing in AI/ML engineering. I approach machine learning as an engineering discipline rather than a research exercise — every project I build is containerized, exposes an API, and handles real-world edge cases such as authentication, rate limiting, and input validation.

My work spans computer vision, natural language processing, retrieval-augmented generation, and agentic LLM pipelines. I have competed in national and AMD-sponsored hackathons with prize pools exceeding $20,000.
